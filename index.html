✦ Our Story ✦

From a Dream to a Blend

TropiGo was born out of a real love for rich, satisfying tropical flavors. We set out to craft the ultimate Creamy Mango Lassi—hitting the perfect balance between sweet, tangy, and silky smooth.

Every cup is freshly blended using real, sun-ripened mangoes carefully sourced from dedicated local farmers, combined with a rich, creamy base that gives it that signature refreshing kick. No artificial fillers—just pure, velvety goodness crafted to support local growers while giving you the ultimate tropical boost.

Whether you need a mid-day energy pick-me-up, a cooling treat on a warm day, or a delicious drink to pair with your favorite meal, TropiGo brings you pure comfort in every sip.

Our mission: To serve fresh, creamy, and delightfully refreshing mango lassi—supporting local fruit farmers, bringing high quality and real flavor, and delivering pure satisfaction to everyone, one cup at a time.

✦ Frequently Asked Questions ✦

Everything You Need to Know

Are your blends 100% natural?

Yes! All TropiGo blends are made with 100% fresh, all-natural fruits. We never use artificial flavors, preservatives, or added sugars.

How much does a TropiGo blend cost?

Our Mango Lassi Classic starts at ₱79 for a 16oz serving. Other flavors range from ₱79 to ₱99.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes! We offer delivery through our partner couriers. You can also choose to pick up your order at our store.

Are your blends healthy?

Absolutely! Each TropiGo blend is packed with vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants. They're naturally sweetened and perfect for a healthy lifestyle.

Do you have vegan options?

Yes, all our fruit blends are plant-based and vegan-friendly. We use coconut or almond milk alternatives upon request.

What are your store hours?

We're open daily from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. You can also order online anytime!
